The piece

Folded light, one pyramid at a time.

522 open paper pyramids stand on a 29 × 18 grid at a 50 mm pitch. Each matte-black shell is paired with a vivid blue shell exactly three quarters its size, tucked against the open side. As the tips rise and lean in a slow wave, the blue appears and disappears behind the black folds.
